****Rating:** Highly Recommended**

**An Eye-Opening and Actionable Framework**<br>[b: Executive Presence: The Missing Link Between Merit and Success|18498555|Executive Presence  The Missing Link Between Merit and Success|Sylvia Ann Hewlett|https://i.gr-assets.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1397422439l/18498555._SY75_.jpg|26186620] by [a: Sylvia Ann Hewlett|10718|Sylvia Ann Hewlett|https://s.gr-assets.com/assets/nophoto/user/u_50x66-632230dc9882b4352d753eedf9396530.png] is a genuinely eye-opening read that provides a practical framework anyone can utilize to advance their career. While executive presence (EP) seems to come effortlessly to some, this book reinforces the encouraging reality that it is entirely learnable for the rest of us. It demystifies the often-vague concept of "leadership material" into tangible, actionable behaviors.

**The Core Challenge: Mastering Gravitas**<br>For me, the most impactful takeaway was the deep dive into **Gravitas**—specifically, the necessity of being assertive and having the ability to "show teeth" when a situation demands it. When directing a team, my default approach has always heavily favored a collaborative, consensus-building style. While collaboration is essential for solving complex problems, this book highlighted exactly why I have historically struggled on the gravitas front. I realized that a purely collaborative style can sometimes inadvertently soften the decisive authority required.

**A Shift in Perspective**<br>Hewlett's insights have fundamentally reshaped my understanding of management fundamentals. Once you internalize the components of EP, you cannot unsee them. You start looking around your organization and noticing exactly why certain people succeed and command respect—they actively project presence. It completely changes how you view professional dynamics, meetings, and success in the workplace.

**What's Next**<br>This book is highly recommended for anyone looking to understand the unwritten rules of professional advancement. To continue building on these fundamentals and directly address my own growth areas, my next read will be [b: Gravitas|18685594|Gravitas  Communicate with Confidence, Influence and Authority|Caroline Goyder|https://i.gr-assets.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1408935677l/18685594._SX50_.jpg|26527196] to dive even deeper into cultivating that specific, assertive command.
