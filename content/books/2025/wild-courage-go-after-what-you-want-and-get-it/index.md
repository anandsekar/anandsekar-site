<!-- REVIEW START -->

Jenny Wood's "Wild Courage" offers a truly refreshing insight into what it takes to be successful, challenging conventional wisdom and advocating for a more authentic, proactive approach. Her "recipe" for success—emphasizing curiosity, overcoming imposter syndrome, daring to be different, and stepping up to lead—provides a compelling framework.

For many, myself included, some of these qualities don't come naturally. The book, however, skillfully provides a different lens through which to view how to be more impactful. It encourages readers to embrace traits often deemed "negative," reframing them as essential acts of courage. This paradigm shift was particularly powerful for me; it made me think in ways I hadn't previously stretched myself, pushing beyond ingrained self-limitations. "Wild Courage" is a genuinely good read for anyone seeking to unlock their full potential.
